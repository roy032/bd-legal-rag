          How this works
          1. Your question is matched against the sections of Bangladeshi Acts published on the Laws of Bangladesh website, in Bangla and English.
          2. The closest sections are given to a language model, which answers only from them and cites each claim as [n].
          3. Automatic checks confirm every citation points to a real excerpt. If the law text does not cover the question, it says so.
